# Illustrations du Sillage — 3 octobre 2026

Trois illustrations générées avec l’outil intégré ImageGen, puis livrées en WebP à trois tailles. Les fichiers consommés par le site sont dans `public/art/`, avec une copie de publication dans `art/`. Les noms sont `sillage-fluide-v1`, `sillage-combat-v1` et `sillage-revers-v1`, avec les suffixes `-medium` et `-small`.

Les images sont des interprétations artistiques. Leur couleur ambrée, les équipements et les formes des lieux ne constituent pas de nouvelles règles du canon. Les figures du combat ne sont pas de nouveaux personnages historiques.

## Prompt — fluide

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a finished landscape 3:2 illustration for MONO's codex of powers. Subject: Sillage, a tangible primordial fluid, NOT generic glowing magic. An intimate cutaway-like view of a sentient stone-and-bronze Golem forearm and open hand above an ancient workshop basin: pearlescent amber liquid threads visibly move in carved capillary channels, pool in translucent ceramic reservoirs, and bridge the hand to a cracked stone it is stabilizing. Show flow, measured pressure, material conduction and deliberate manipulation. The Golem hand has nonhuman engineered articulation and weathered mineral anatomy. One clearly readable composition, physical fluid with meniscus and fine currents, macro material detail, dark teal shadow and restrained warm light compatible with a literary French dark fantasy codex. Medium: exquisite mineral engraving combined with translucent ink washes and realistic sculptural texture. No text, no labels, no UI, no runes masquerading as text, no watermark, no armor warrior, no laser beams. Output save as an image artifact for a website.

## Prompt — combat

Use case: stylized-concept. Finished landscape 3:2 narrative illustration for MONO codex Powerscaling. Depict a tactical battle as intelligible manipulation of a tangible primordial fluid called Sillage. Wide view at ground level of a fractured ancient stone bridge under siege. Foreground: one small human practitioner in practical travel clothes deliberately guides liquid amber currents through the grooves of a spear; its thin coherent blade is formed from flowing fluid. Midground: a massive nonhuman geological Anakim, strata and roots rather than human armor, anchors a translucent liquid membrane into bridge stones to protect a few retreating figures. A projectile visibly bends through a paired oval threshold off to the side; the physical bridge supports and anchored liquid conduits are crucial to the scene. No triumphant superhero poses, no generic duel of identical wizards. Emphasize unequal capacities, teamwork, preparation and costly defense. Medium: epic painterly oil illustration with strong brush texture, restrained teal slate, luminous warm amber fluid, storm light, legible silhouettes and generous atmospheric depth. No text, charts, labels, numbers, runes, UI or watermark. Do not portray the deities or add invented major lore figures.

## Prompt — inversions

Use case: stylized-concept. A finished landscape 3:2 illustration for MONO's seven abyssal inversions, a visual grammar DIFFERENT from the warm realistic fluid workshop and epic battle. Create a metaphysical nonfigurative scene as a dark intaglio print and translucent lacquer painting: a single continuous pearlescent amber primordial liquid stream crosses a fractured obsidian chamber, encountering seven visibly distinct transformations along its winding path. One portion sealed behind an opaque mineral lattice (enclosure); one obscured under blank black film (ignorance); one leaves an erased pale trace (forgetting); one swells past a cracked vessel's capacity (excess); one is diverted by hooked branching black conduits (possession); one seems to continue only inside a false reflected chamber while real fluid pools below (illusion); one corrodes its supporting structure into fine mineral flakes (corruption). Elegant coherent spatial composition, not seven panels, not a chart. Deep indigo and black, silver-white engraved textures, small amber liquid highlights. Express loss of direction and compromised circulation physically, beautiful and unsettling. No demon lineup, no humanoid villain, no fireball, no text, no glyphs, no labels, no watermark, no UI. The same fluid remains recognizable throughout.
